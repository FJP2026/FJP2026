Hi, I'm Farzaneh Jalalypour.

Data scientist and ML engineer in Gothenburg, Sweden, working on AI for life science and clinical data.

I have a PhD in computational biology and five years of research at Chalmers and KTH / SciLifeLab, applying machine learning and generative AI to biological and clinical data.

See my projects LinkedIn

About me

I work across the full data science workflow in Python: preparing data, building models with PyTorch, TensorFlow and scikit-learn, evaluating them, and turning the work into reproducible pipelines. Recent projects include benchmarking generative AI tools for protein structure prediction, LLM-based extraction of data from scientific literature, and an AI recommender system for clinical trial design with AstraZeneca and Evinova.

Before data science, I worked in the pharmaceutical industry and in a medical research lab. That background helps me speak the language of clinicians, researchers and business stakeholders, and I enjoy explaining complex results clearly. I have lectured to around 250 students per period and supervised master's theses with industry partners.
